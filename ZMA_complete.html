Before you ask

Questions we
get a lot.

Anything else, message us on WhatsApp. We answer 24 hours a day, seven days a week.

How long does a course take?

ZTF courses are accelerated. Most run between one and two weeks. Online, you work through the same material at your own pace and keep access afterwards.

Do I need to read music to start?

No. The beginner level starts with introduction to the instrument, musical basics, reading, rhythm and posture. Reading is taught, not assumed.

Do I have to be a Christian to enrol?

The academy is a Christian institution and the teaching is shaped by that, including spiritual foundations at beginner level and ministry work at advanced level. Anyone willing to learn in that setting is welcome to register.

What ages do you teach?

Children, teenagers and adults, each with their own track. Private lessons are available at any age for targeted, personalised progress.

Do I need my own instrument?

High quality equipment is available to students at no extra cost, and the academy has acoustically treated studios for rehearsal and recording.
